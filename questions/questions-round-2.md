## What changed since round 1

You said capturing the answers matters more in the long run. Agreed, and the
design now reflects it: each process carries a **`description`** field — two to
five sentences on what it is, who does it, how it actually runs, and what breaks
— plus **`description_source`** recording who said it and when.

For most of our processes that paragraph *is* the documentation. The Standards
test for "Operational" is *written, assigned, consistently implemented*; a
paragraph and a named owner satisfies the first two. Only genuinely complex
processes need a separate SOP behind a link.

**So Part 2 below is the important part of this round.** Parts 1 and 3 are
quick; Part 2 is where the durable value is, and it's the part nobody but you
can currently write.

One boundary worth holding: **a description is a paragraph, never a manual.** If
an answer wants headings and numbered steps, it's a document — say so and I'll
link it rather than inline it.
