# Renewal, Insurance and Compliance Calendar

_Source: JR Logan, 2026-08-14 (process registry round 2). Owner: Executive
Director. Review annually._

The recurring insurance policies, property agreements and regulatory filings
MakeHaven must keep current. Until this document existed, the whole calendar
lived in one person's head and the trigger for every item was an inbound email —
which is how the 990 was filed late in 2025 and incurred a fine.

**Process registry:** `insurance_compliance.renewals`, `governance.records_retention`

## 3. Compliance and tax filings

### CT Secretary of the State Annual Report
- **Due:** March (target filing 26 March)
- **Where:** Business.CT.gov, Business Services Division
- **Details:** filed online using ALEI `US-CT.BER:1066239`. $50 fee.

### CPA financial review preparation
- **Window:** April–June. Request list arrives late April; draft review
  completed mid-June.
- **Vendor:** Zackin Zimyeski Sullivan CPA LLC — Keith Sullivan, Emma West
  (`ewest@zzscpa.com`)
- **Prepare:** prior-year bank statements, 1099s, board minutes, fixed asset
  additions and disposals, payroll summaries, adjusting journal entries.

### IRS Form 990
- **Statutory deadline:** 15 May. **Routinely extended to 15 November** by
  filing Form 8868 electronically in May.
- **Prepared by:** ZZSCPA, in tandem with the financial review.
- **Signed by:** Board Treasurer.
- ⚠️ Late filing in 2025 resulted in a fine. The extension filing in May is the
  control that prevents this — it is not optional paperwork.

### CT Charitable Solicitation Registration
- **Renews:** annually, per the state portal schedule
- **Agency:** CT Department of Consumer Protection, Public Charities Unit
- An active "CHR" registration number is **required** for state and municipal
  grant applications and for public fundraising. Lapsing it blocks grant
  submissions.

### Board conflict-of-interest disclosures
- **Collected:** July / mid-year, and on onboarding for new directors
- **Owner:** Board President / Executive Director
- Signed electronically at
  [makehaven.org/conflict-interest-policy](https://www.makehaven.org/conflict-interest-policy).

## Known gaps

- **No records-retention policy exists.** This is a Standards of Excellence
  requirement in its own right and blocks the three-years-of-evidence test for
  the top recognition level.
- **No reminder mechanism.** Every item above is currently triggered by an
  inbound email from the vendor or agency. A calendar with lead times — or the
  automated check JR has wanted — would convert this from reactive to
  scheduled. The May 8868 extension is the highest-value one to automate first.
